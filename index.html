Apple Health and Health Connect

Your sets and watch data in one report.

Your watch records heart rate and activity while you log sets in Dayily. With your permission, the app can add available Apple Health or Health Connect data to your workout report.

01

One training history

View runs, rides, and other workouts from Apple Health or Health Connect alongside the sessions you record in Dayily.

02

More detail about your workout

Dayily can match your session with an activity recorded at the same time and add duration, distance, heart rate, and energy expenditure to the report.

03

With your permission

Available measurements depend on your device, platform, permissions, and what your watch or band records.

Free and Premium

Keep your training history close.

Your workouts are stored on your phone and available offline. Logging workouts and keeping your full history on the device is free. Premium expands cloud storage and sync.

Free

For logging workouts and keeping your history on your phone.

  • Full workout history on your device
  • Core workout journal features
  • Cloud storage and sync with limits

Premium

For keeping more workouts in the cloud and syncing between devices.

  • More workouts in your cloud history

  • Local history remains free to access
