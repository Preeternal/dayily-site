Delete your Dayily account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Dayily account and the Dayily data associated with it.

Delete your account in the app

You can permanently delete your Dayily account directly in the Dayily app:

Settings → Cloud Account → Delete account and data

This permanently deletes your Dayily account, local workouts for this profile, and associated cloud data.

If you cannot access the app

Send an account deletion request to dayily.review@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Dayily account. Please include enough information for us to identify the account.

Data managed by other services

Data independently stored by Apple Health, Health Connect, Google Play, or the App Store is not deleted by this request. Those records and accounts must be managed through the corresponding service.

Deleting data without deleting the account

Dayily currently does not offer a separate request to delete some or all server-side data while keeping the Dayily account. Individual workouts can be deleted in the app, but full account and associated cloud data deletion is part of deleting the account.
